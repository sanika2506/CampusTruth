What makes a good claim?

  • Specific: “Library closes at 4pm on Friday” beats “Library is closing.”
  • Checkable: Include a date, place, or department when you can.
  • Respectful: Keep personal allegations out of the demo.
  • Honest: Say what you know — and what you do not.
